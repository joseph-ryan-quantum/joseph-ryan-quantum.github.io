We report the first experimental measurement of quantum first-passage-time distributions (QFPTDs), using a motional mode of a single trapped ion. We developed a novel composite-phase laser pulse sequence to perform tunable stroboscopic single-shot projective measurements of the ion's motional state, and measured QFPTDs of the ion energy under electric-field noise, establishing a clear connection to the classical first-passage-time distribution. This opens a new avenue for experimental study of quantum first-passage processes, with relevance to quantum search algorithms, quantum-classical correspondence, and the quantum measurement problem.

A preprint is available on [arXiv:2508.21790](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21790).
